WHEEL-SEAM-02A — triangle/mesh seam evidence
Date: 2026-08-04
Base HEAD before this checkpoint: 8472512 (WHEEL-RIGID-01)
Scope: b3Wheel vs one-sided finite triangle boundaries and mesh seams.
Not in scope: full wheel-vs-hull face clipping / edge-axis SAT, local tire softness, manual driving feel.

PROTOCOL
- Red tests were written before each corresponding implementation change.
- Coplanar loaded seam: both directions, wheel phases 0.00 / 0.73 / 1.61 rad.
- Shallow folded seam: 0.02 m rise over 1.00 m (~1.15 deg), both directions, phases 0.00 / 1.17 rad.
- Coplanar acceptance: no contact gap, exactly one solver point, normal impulse within 5%, stable featureId, exactly one triangle handoff, at most one persisted reset.
- Folded acceptance: no contact gap, 1-2 points allowed for two distinct face normals, adjacent solved normals dot > 0.995, total impulse within 20% after warm-up, stable featureId, exactly one triangle handoff, at most one persisted reset.
- The 20% folded threshold was fixed after exploratory pre-fix measurements (+9% uphill, +13.5% downhill) and before the representative-normal correction. It is not a threshold tuned on the final green output.

RED-BEFORE-GREEN OBSERVATIONS
1. Finite triangle edge: the infinite-plane support existed, but barycentric filtering removed every point; the new edge fallback test failed because pointCount was 0 instead of 1.
2. Back face: b3Wheel generated contact from the non-authored side; the one-sided negative regression test failed because pointCount was nonzero.
3. Shallow folded seam, phase 1.17 rad: the mesh cluster kept the first (flat) triangle normal while the deeper point already belonged to the ramp. Immediately after the late normal switch, total normal impulse was 12.922590 N*s versus the flat reference 9.472924232 N*s, a +36.416% spike.
