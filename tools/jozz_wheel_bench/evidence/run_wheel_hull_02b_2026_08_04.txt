FINAL ALGORITHM CONTRACT
- Exact hull face axes and finite clipping of wheel-plane points to the face polygon.
- Conservative fast return only when the entire wheel projection lies inside every side plane of the face prism.
- Deterministic feature walk over the hull graph, each valid half-edge pair evaluated at most once.
- Numerical edge normal-cone search: 4 coarse samples + 5 ternary refinements.
- Numerical vertex normal-cone search: 9 deterministic convex-blend iterations.
- Stable, disjoint face/edge/vertex feature-pair encoding.
- This is a controlled bounded numerical search, not a claim of an analytic exact SAT proof.
